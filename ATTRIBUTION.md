## Portraits

Twenty official congressional portrait images were downloaded from [unitedstates/images](https://github.com/unitedstates/images), using each member's Bioguide ID and the `225x275` size. Its [README](https://github.com/unitedstates/images/blob/gh-pages/README.md) identifies congressional portraits as public domain and dedicates other repository files under CC0. The image filenames preserve the Bioguide IDs. No endorsement is implied.

The chamber illustration and interface icons are original code-native SVGs. The chamber illustration is decorative and does not depict a particular seat count or party distribution.
