# Member question bank source checks

Reviewed November 13, 2026. Twenty original questions and twenty study profiles. All questions use historical, dated actions rather than assumed current offices or latest bill status. Profile focus lines describe the selected record, not a complete ideology or current committee assignment. Party and state identify the members in these records.

Sources were read through web search results and primary-page retrieval. Official bill metadata is used for sponsors, original cosponsors and introduced versions. Member/committee releases are used as evidence of their own statements and actions, not as proof of contested claims about companies. No external quiz question text was copied.

The Sanders/Ocasio-Cortez item describes their March 25 announcement, not the later introduced bill date. The moratorium item concerns state-law preemption; it must not be conflated with a data-center moratorium. The Blumenthal–Hawley framework is not treated as enacted law. No AI report/roadmap is treated as law.

No current pending-status claim is made. Additional source review is required to turn these historical questions into live-status questions.
